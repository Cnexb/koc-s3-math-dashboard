Special lines, congruence & similarity, angle pairs, and the four centres — with standard geometry markings.

Lab 1 · Special lines & centres

Four special lines → four centres

All construction lines are solid. Medians use tick marks on equal halves; altitudes show a right-angle symbol at the foot; angle bisectors use outward arc marks.

Lab 2 · Congruent triangles

SSS · SAS · ASA · AAS · RHS

Tick marks show equal lengths; arc marks show equal angles. Pick a condition to see what must match.

Lab 3 · Angle pairs (parallel lines)

Corresponding · alternate · interior on same side

Two parallel lines cut by a transversal — highlighted angles share the chosen relationship.

Lab 4 · Similar triangles

AAA · three sides proportional · two sides + included angle

Similar triangles have equal angles and proportional sides. Adjust the scale factor \(k\).
