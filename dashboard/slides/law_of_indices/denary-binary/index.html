Denary → Binary

Peel off powers of 2 from \(23_{10}\):

23 \(= 16 + 7\) → \(1\times2^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{4}} +\) 7
7 \(= 4 + 3\) → \(1\times2^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{2}} +\) 3
3 \(= 2 + 1\) → \(1\times2^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{1}} + 1\times2^{\textcolor{#ffd54f}{0}}\)
\(23_{10}=10111_{2}\)
